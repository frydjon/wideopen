**I love how your work feels like it's steeped in nostalgia. What were some of your earliest memories of visual media?**

**Nina:** "a pirated copy of shrek 2, westerns with my Nana and Rehka with my Nani"

**Athen:** "a pirated copy of an animation about a duck who gets lost, I have no idea what it was, my dad showing me the audition (1999) aged 11"

**N+A:** "wacky races box set"

**In that nostalgic aspect of your work is a use of material that feels like an homage to school years. Is that intentional?**

**N+A:** "school was funny and terrible and explosive and, well, completely formative, but more often than not a nightmare"

**On materials & intention:**

The artists explain their use of accessible, everyday materials as a deliberate practice of deconstructing identity formation and re-contextualizing personal history through familiar objects found in homes, schools, and workplaces—rejecting exclusionary "high art" materials in favor of intuitive, relatable creation.
